# Reporting-Guideline Lens

Identify only guidelines relevant to the stated study design or assay, such as TRIPOD-family guidance for prediction, STARD-family guidance for diagnostic accuracy, MIAME for microarrays, MINSEQE for sequencing, or FAIR principles for reusable data. Audit report completeness without presenting a checklist as proof of scientific validity. If current guideline text is unavailable, return cannot_verify rather than guessing requirements.
