# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, evidence-focused community across disciplines, career stages, countries, identities, and levels of programming experience.

Contributors must discuss methods and evidence without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or disclosure of private research materials. Scientific disagreement is welcome when it is specific, constructive, and proportionate to the evidence.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
